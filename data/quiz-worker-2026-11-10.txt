Q+A Katha - Reading 🧠

Question: How many people can you recruit in the game Suikoden in a single playthrough?

Answer: 107 - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Reading pathi - Answer 107. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Reading nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Reading oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Reading pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Reading ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Reading nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Reading ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
